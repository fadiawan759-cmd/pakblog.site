PAKBLOG 8.0 — DEPLOY CHECKLIST

1. Upload the contents of this ZIP to the Netlify site serving pakblog.site.
2. Ensure Netlify environment variable FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON is present for the public server functions.
3. Deploy.
4. Test in a private/incognito tab:
   /
   /search?q=US
   /latest
   /category/technology
   /sitemap.xml
   /rss.xml
   /about
   /news/<one-published-slug>
5. In Google Search Console, open the submitted sitemap and use REQUEST AGAIN after the new deployment is live.

The sitemap fallback means Google can still read the XML even during a temporary Firebase outage; when Firebase is available, published article URLs are included automatically.
