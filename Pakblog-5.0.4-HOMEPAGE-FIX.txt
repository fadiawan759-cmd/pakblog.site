PAKBLOG 5.0.4 — HOMEPAGE VISUAL FIX

This build keeps the working Firebase/Netlify server-rendered news system and fixes the homepage presentation:
- Broken/missing article images no longer fall back to the Pakblog logo, which previously made the logo appear huge inside the hero.
- Added a dedicated news-placeholder.svg for missing images.
- Expanded image field compatibility (imageURL, imageUrl, heroImage, heroImageUrl, featuredImage, coverImage, thumbnail, image, photoUrl).
- Reduced mobile hero title sizing/spacing so long headlines do not overpower the layout.

The Firebase service-account environment variable remains required in Netlify:
FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON
